Continuity of Integrity Protocol

The Legacy Preservation Clause

The Koinonia Grace Foundation (KGF) shall remain perpetually aligned with the ethical standards established by Thin Black and Brown Line USA (Tbblusa.org). Any deviation from the "Protector Standard" in the verification process shall trigger an immediate review by the Board of Trustees.

Leadership Transition Protocol

"In the event of a leadership vacancy, the Interim Executive Director must be selected from the pool of Tier 3 Verified Protectors with a minimum of 24 months of active service within the Philadelphia ecosystem."

Asset Protection & Data Sovereignty

The Institutional Intelligence Dashboard and the Verification Repository are the intellectual property of KGF. These assets may not be sold, traded, or shared with commercial entities, ensuring that the safety of Philadelphia’s youth is never a commodity.

Dissolution Directive

Should the foundation cease operations, all remaining funds and digital assets shall be transferred to a 501(c)(3) entity that maintains a primary focus on the protection of underserved youth, as verified by the Tbblusa Heritage Committee.
