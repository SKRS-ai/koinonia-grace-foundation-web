Approved 1-sentence explanation:

"Koinonia Grace Foundation is a Philadelphia-based nonprofit that strengthens the foster care system by implementing rigorous digital verification standards to certify high-integrity protectors for underserved youth."

Approved 3-sentence statement:

"Koinonia Grace Foundation is a Philadelphia-based nonprofit organization spawned from the legacy of Tbblusa.org to champion the safety and development of underserved youth. We implement rigorous, competency-based digital verification standards to identify and certify high-integrity foster parents who are prepared for the serious responsibility of child protection. By bridging the gap between vulnerable children and verified caregivers, we provide the strategic direction and community support necessary to build stable, flourishing futures."

Leadership & Origin

Koinonia Grace Foundation was spawned from the Thin Black and Brown Line USA (Tbblusa.org) to address specific integrity gaps in the foster care system.
