From Tbblusa.org to Koinonia Grace

Koinonia Grace Foundation is a Philadelphia-based nonprofit organization spawned from the legacy of Tbblusa.org to champion the safety and development of underserved youth. We leverage the strength of the Thin Black and Brown Line USA to create a secure, disciplined, and high-integrity environment for children in need of foster care.

The Philadelphia Challenge

In our community, the foster care system faces a dire need for more than just beds—it needs verified protectors. We identify whether a person is properly fit to be a foster parent by offering verification and certifications that ensure they possess what is needed to lead, not just collect a check.
