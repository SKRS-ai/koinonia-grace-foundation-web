Effective Date: March 2026

Koinonia Grace Foundation operates under the moral and ethical lineage of Tbblusa.org. By accessing our verification products, you agree to our High-Integrity Standard.

1. Verification Terms

The Koinonia Grace Foundation ("KGF") provides digital certification and verification products to identify foster care readiness.

Integrity Levels

Completion of a KGF verification product does not guarantee placement of a child by Philadelphia DHS or any other agency. KGF identifies "Integrity Levels" based on self-reported data and specialized training metrics.

Revocation

KGF reserves the right to revoke any digital badge or certification if a member is found to be in violation of the Institutional Ethics Code.

2. Privacy & Data Protection

We take the security of Philadelphia’s protectors seriously. All personal data is encrypted according to institutional government standards.

  • Identity Protection: We do not sell foster parent data to third-party marketers.
  • Transparency: Data is only shared with authorized government entities or grant partners when explicitly authorized by the member.

3. Non-Transactional Care

All members acknowledge that foster care is a service of protection, not a commercial transaction. Any attempt to use KGF certifications to facilitate fraudulent financial gain will result in immediate termination of membership and a report to the proper authorities.
