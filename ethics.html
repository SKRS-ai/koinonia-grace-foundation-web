Institutional Protocol | Koinonia Grace Foundation

I. The Heritage Mandate

As an entity spawned from Thin Black and Brown Line USA (Tbblusa.org), the Koinonia Grace Foundation operates under a mandate of uncompromising integrity. Our staff and representatives are the physical embodiment of the "Protector" standard.

II. Data Sanctity & Child Welfare

All member data regarding Philadelphia foster candidates is considered Institutional Priority Alpha. Unauthorized disclosure of vetting results or background details is grounds for immediate termination and legal referral.

III. The Protector’s Creed

  • 1. Objectivity: We vet based on competency and integrity, never on personal bias.
  • 2. Vigilance: We acknowledge that our digital products are the gatekeepers of child safety.
  • 3. Non-Transactionalism: We refuse any incentive that compromises the quality of a placement.

IV. Institutional Review

The Ethics Committee shall review the Verification Shop algorithms annually to ensure they align with evolving Philadelphia child safety laws and 501(c)(3) compliance standards.
